



复杂的计算量：
   ① A1,...,Ak 的组合
           ↓
   ② 每个 Aη 选择 Ci
           ↓
   ③ 得到大量 Gamma branch


① Atom Set
   ↓
   subset pruning

② Support Set
   ↓
   提前计算 Dec + cache

③ Constraint Set
   ↓
   duplicate + subset pruning


TBox Atoms
    ↓
Atom Set
    ↓
minimal premise sets
    ↓
Support Sets
    ↓
Delta Sets
    ↓
minimal delta family
    ↓
Gamma branches


ConceptPatternNode immutable + canonicalization
        ↓
Dec cache
        ↓
Subsumption cache
        ↓
TBox atom set
        ↓
Set-based Mutation




/*

 * ================================================================
 * Set-based Mutation Optimization
 * ================================================================
 *
 * Original target:
 *
 *     C1 ∩ ... ∩ Cn  <=?  D
 *
 * ------------------------------------------------
 * Phase 1: Build usable TBox atoms
 * ------------------------------------------------
 *
 *     Atoms(T)
 *         |
 *         v
 *     canonical Set
 *         |
 *         v
 *     compute Support(A)
 *
 *     Support(A)
 *       = { Ci | Dec(Ci,A) succeeds }
 *
 *         |
 *         v
 *
 *     remove A if Support(A) = empty
 *
 *         |
 *         v
 *
 *     SupportedAtoms
 *
 *
 * ------------------------------------------------
 * Phase 2: Search premise SETS
 * ------------------------------------------------
 *
 *     choose B
 *         |
 *         v
 *     Dec(B,D)
 *
 *      failure ------> reject B
 *
 *      success
 *         |
 *         v
 *
 *     search:
 *
 *         P subset-of SupportedAtoms
 *
 *     such that:
 *
 *         intersection(P) <=_T B
 *
 *
 *     If P succeeds:
 *
 *         P
 *         |
 *         v
 *       keep P
 *         |
 *         X
 *
 *     Do not search supersets of P.
 *
 *
 *     If even the maximal extension fails:
 *
 *         intersection(P union Remaining) !<=_T B
 *
 *     then:
 *
 *         prune the whole subtree.
 *
 *
 * ------------------------------------------------
 * Phase 3: Generate branch constraint SETS
 * ------------------------------------------------
 *
 *     P = {A1,...,Ak}
 *
 *          |
 *          v
 *
 *     Support(A1) x ... x Support(Ak)
 *
 *          |
 *          v
 *
 *     Dec-generated constraint sets
 *
 *          |
 *          v
 *
 *     Delta1, Delta2, ...
 *
 *
 * ------------------------------------------------
 * Phase 4: Minimize branch SETS
 * ------------------------------------------------
 *
 *     Delta1 = Delta2
 *
 *         -> remove duplicate
 *
 *
 *     Delta1 subset Delta2
 *
 *         -> keep Delta1
 *         -> remove Delta2
 *
 *
 *          |
 *          v
 *
 *     MinimalDeltaFamily
 *
 *
 * ------------------------------------------------
 * Phase 5: DFS
 * ------------------------------------------------
 *
 *     one minimal Delta
 *          |
 *          v
 *     one Gamma branch
 *          |
 *          v
 *         DFS
 *
 * ================================================================
 */
